PRIVACY POLICY

Your thoughts are not an advertising product.

Plain-language summary

You can use Itjima without an account. Guest data stays in your browser. If you sign in, your content is stored with our service providers so it can sync across devices. We do not sell your saved thoughts or schedules, and we do not use their contents for advertising. You can export or delete your data from Settings → Data & privacy.

1. Information we collect

When you use Itjima without signing in

Thoughts, tasks, schedules, archive items, preferences, and related app state are stored locally in your browser or installed web app. They are not synced to an Itjima account unless you choose to sign in.

When you create or use an account

  • Account identifiers, such as email address, user ID, and authentication provider.
  • Content you submit, including thoughts, tasks, schedules, archive items, and optional images.
  • Schedule metadata, including dates, times, time zone, repeat settings, and reminder settings.
  • Notification registration data needed to deliver web push notifications to your device.
  • Feedback you send, including the message, optional email, page path, and basic browser information.
  • Technical logs needed to secure, diagnose, and operate the service.

Do not store passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, or other secrets in Itjima. Natural-language input can contain sensitive information; you are responsible for deciding what to save.

2. How we use information

  • Provide capture, schedule, archive, sync, and reminder features.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Maintain data integrity, prevent abuse, and recover from service failures.
  • Respond to feedback and support requests.
  • Improve reliability, accessibility, and product usability using aggregated or limited diagnostic information.

Itjima does not use the contents of your saved thoughts, schedules, or archive items to build advertising profiles or target ads.

3. Service providers and disclosure

We use service providers only as needed to operate Itjima, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, synchronization, and server functions.
  • Vercel for web hosting, delivery, and operational infrastructure.
  • Browser and device push services when you choose to enable notifications.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell personal information for money. If Itjima later adds advertising or cross-context behavioral tracking, this policy and the product controls will be updated before that change is enabled.

4. Your controls and rights

  • Access and portability: download an export from Settings → Data & privacy.
  • Correction: edit or replace your saved content and schedule details in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual items, local guest data, or your account and associated server data.
  • Notifications: disable notifications in Itjima and in your browser or operating-system settings.
  • Feedback and requests: open Itjima, tap the ITJIMA brand, and choose Send feedback.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to know, access, correct, delete, or limit certain processing. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

5. Retention and security

Guest data remains on your device until you delete it, clear browser storage, or uninstall the web app. Account data is kept while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Account deletion removes active account data through the deletion workflow; limited security logs or provider backups may remain temporarily under provider retention cycles.

We use authenticated access, row-level authorization, encrypted network transport, and restricted server credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so keep an independent copy of critical schedules and do not store secrets in Itjima.

6. Children

Itjima is not directed to children under 13 in the United States, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through in-app feedback so it can be reviewed and removed.

7. International use

Itjima may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Service providers may operate infrastructure in multiple regions. By using the service, you understand that information may be transferred and processed under different data-protection laws.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product and service providers change. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be communicated in the product when practical. For privacy questions or requests, open Itjima, tap the ITJIMA brand, and choose Send feedback.

This policy describes the current public beta. It is written to make product behavior clear and does not limit rights provided by applicable law.
